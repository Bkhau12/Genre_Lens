MovieLens 32M sample: ratings from 2020-01-01 onward
=====================================================

200,000 ratings sampled at random (seed 42) from the 238,669 ratings dated 2020-01-01 or later
in the ml-32m-2000-2023-1m sample. MovieLens 32M ratings end in October 2023, so this set covers
2020-01-01 to 2023-10-13. movies.csv keeps only the 14,043 films these ratings use.

Columns are unchanged from MovieLens (userId, movieId, rating, timestamp / movieId, title, genres).

Source: F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan. 2015. The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context.
ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems (TiiS) 5, 4: 19:1-19:19. https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/32m/
